A life of

I had a real hunger to explore from a young age. Always curious. Never still. Eager to discover new places and follow the path less trodden. I love hiking because I get to see new things, be in the wild outdoors. Hiking clears your mind.

02 / Me

Built to
explore

Based in Bellpuig, a small town in the Lleida region of Catalonia. By day, I'm a backend developer at BonArea in Guissona, building the systems that keep things running. When the workday ends, you'll find me out on the trails — running or cycling through the backroads and paths of the Catalan countryside.

I race trail marathons and backyard ultras, chasing horizons one step at a time. The trails teach patience. The code teaches precision. Together, they keep me balanced.
